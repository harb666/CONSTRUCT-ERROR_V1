# CONSTRUCT-ERROR

Third-person mobile co-op arena shooter (Godot 4.7, GL Compatibility renderer).
Current state: **movement prototype only**.

## Play the latest build
https://harb666.github.io/CONSTRUCT-ERROR_V1/  (rotate phone to landscape)

Every push to `main` or `claude/**` runs `.github/workflows/web-build.yml` (~3 min):
import → Web export → the full test suite twice at the same time (on the project, and on the exported `index.pck`), in fast-forward (`--fixed-fps 60`: every frame is 1/60 s of game time, simulated as fast as the CPU allows - ~1 min instead of ~5.5 min in real time) → publish. Nothing is published unless both runs end with `FAILURES: 0`.
Publishing: the export is pushed to the `gh-pages` branch (live when Settings → Pages → Source is "Deploy from a branch"; kept as the rollback) and also deployed directly by the `pages` job (live when the source is "GitHub Actions" - no separate branch build; it fails softly otherwise).
Run the suite locally the same way: `godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . -s tests/movement_smoke_test.gd`. Tests that depend on the wall clock (the weapon wheel's easing) wait for it with `_wall_wait`.
The HUD shows the branch/commit of the running build.

## Controls
| Touch | Keyboard / pad |
|---|---|
| Left side: floating move stick - light push walks, firmer push runs, all the way sprints (for as long as it's held there) | WASD (full = sprint) / left stick |
| Right side: drag to look | mouse drag / right stick |
| JUMP (tap again in air = double jump) | Space / A |
| DODGE (direction = stick, else facing; a ~0.34 s slide that ends in a small kick of dust) | Q or Ctrl / B |
| (no button: push the stick all the way to sprint) | Shift (hold) / RT also sprints |
| Weapon button (hold, drag to a weapon, release) | Tab (hold) + mouse |

Gaits from the stick (`PlayerController`): pushed up to `walk_zone` (0.5) it walks (up to `walk_speed` 4 m/s), further it blends into a run (`run_min_speed` 6.2 .. `run_speed` 8.5 m/s), and from `sprint_zone` (0.9) it sprints at `sprint_speed` 11.5 m/s until the push drops below `sprint_exit` (0.82; no flicker at the edge). Speeds line up with the Walking / Running / RunFast clips.

## Level: Toxic Arena
The game runs in **Toxic Arena** (`assets/levels/toxic_arena/level.glb`, made by [image-to-level](https://github.com/harb666/image-to-level), imported unchanged at 1:1 scale). `scripts/world/toxic_arena.gd` adds what it needs to be played: collision from its named meshes (boxes, plus convex hulls for the ramps, stairs and the chamfered central platform; none on decoration), a HazardFluid kill volume (player back to spawn, robots die; a hidden basin floor catches debris) and an out-of-bounds volume below the level. ## Layout
- `scripts/input/` – `PlayerCommand` (one tick of intent, serialisable), `PlayerInput` (source base class), `LocalPlayerInput` (this device).
- `scripts/player/player_controller.gd` – movement; reads only its `PlayerInput`, no globals. `simulate(cmd, dt)` is the single tick step.
- `scripts/camera/camera_rig.gd` – per-local-player orbit camera; owns view yaw used for camera-relative movement.
- `scripts/ui/touch_controls.gd` – multi-touch stick / look / buttons.
- `scripts/character/character_animator.gd` – builds the AnimationTree state machine and drives it from the controller (visual only, no root motion).
- `scripts/character/arm_aim_modifier.gd` – procedural upper-body layer: raises both arms so the forearm cannons point forward while running/sprinting (re-poses only the existing UpperArm/ForeArm bones at runtime).
- `scripts/character/hips_corrector.gd` – runtime-only Hips pose fix-up that keeps clips with baked travel/turning in place.
- `assets/characters/grinch/grinch.glb` – source character (28-joint Mixamo rig; never modified). Textures are extracted next to it and imported at 1024 px.
- `scripts/main.gd` – `spawn_player(id, is_local)`; the future network layer plugs in here.
- `tests/movement_smoke_test.gd` – `godot --headless --path . -s tests/movement_smoke_test.gd`

## Weapons (pickup/spawn system)
- `WeaponDefinition` (`scripts/weapons/weapon_definition.gd`, instances in `resources/weapons/`) – data for one weapon: scene, mount bone/side/offset, pickup display height. New weapons = new definition + weapon scene; no new pickup code.
- `Weapon` (`scripts/weapons/weapon.gd`) – base for weapon scenes; finds standard markers (`Arm_Socket_Attachment`, `Muzzle_Exit`).
- `WeaponSpawnPad` (pad prefab root) – set its `weapon` to any definition on each placed pad; its `WeaponSpawner` child floats, spins and bobs the weapon and hands it to the first player with a `WeaponHolder` entering the zone (`respawn_time` optional).
- `WeaponHolder` (on the player) – **dual wield controller**: a RIGHT and a LEFT `WeaponSlot` (`scripts/weapons/weapon_slot.gd`). Each slot owns its weapon, target lock, aiming, auto-fire, cooldown (the weapon's), recoil and swapping; changing one never touches the other. A slot equips onto its forearm bone via `BoneAttachment3D` (the definition's `mount_bone` side is swapped to match; roll and `mount_out` mirrored), keeps the socket on the forearm axis (+`mount_lift` up, `mount_out` outward) and the weapon upright each frame. Armed arms are held raised by the arm-aim layer. `equip(def, side)`, `unequip(side, restore_default)`, `weapon(side)`; `current` = RIGHT weapon. The holder has no weapon-specific logic.
- **Weapon arm attachment standard** (all current and future weapons): the character has no hands; weapons replace them. Each arm has a character-side `WeaponSocket` (`RightWeaponSocket` / `LeftWeaponSocket`, created by the slots) on the forearm axis, `CharacterAnimator.weapon_socket_offset` (0.05 m) past the elbow, kept upright - exactly the Black Hole Generator's mount, the master reference (its placement is verified unchanged to 1e-6 m). Weapons choose how they meet it (`WeaponDefinition.mount_mode`): `SLEEVE` - the weapon's `Arm_Socket_Attachment` sits on the socket and the weapon swallows the forearm (BHG); `ARM_END` - the weapon's rear plugs `arm_end_insert` (0.04 m) into the open end of the forearm gauntlet (`arm_end_offset` 0.34 m past the socket, measured from the mesh) and extends forward only, centred on the arm (cannon, shotgun; their rears are smaller than the 0.24 x 0.27 m gauntlet, so they come out of its end rather than sleeving it). Any held-weapon geometry behind that boundary is trimmed off the held copy (shared trimmed meshes; pads show the whole weapon), and recoil can never slide a weapon back past it. Weapons are never scaled or distorted to fit; `mount_offset`/`mount_lift`/`mount_out`/`mount_roll_degrees` are only small per-weapon nudges (all 0 now).
- Default weapon: `default_weapon` (`resources/weapons/default_cannon.tres`) goes into BOTH slots at spawn. Pickups (pads) go to the RIGHT slot; the LEFT keeps its default cannon.
- Plasma Cannon (`scenes/weapons/default_cannon.tscn`, `PlasmaCannon`): arm-mounted, riding on top/outside of each forearm. Fires cyan `PlasmaBolt`s (4/s, 0.45 damage, 42 m/s; same pooled bolts/effects as the robots, recoloured, flying through players) with a cyan muzzle flash and light; light recoil (0.3). `pistol_cannon.glb` is the supplied `Pistol_cannon_v1.glb` simplified 355k → 23k vertices (visually identical); the source (`pistol_cannon_source*`) stays in the repo but is excluded from export. Textures imported at 1024 px.
- Shotgun (`scenes/weapons/shotgun.tscn`, `Shotgun`, `resources/weapons/shotgun.tres`): five barrels (`Muzzle_1..5` markers at the measured bore centres), every shot fires all five. Each barrel emits its own spiral energy stream (`ShotgunStream`, pooled: hot core streak + double helix of orange motes, 170 m/s so it feels instant but is visible) and the hit (one ray per stream, decided at firing) lands when the stream arrives. Spread grows smoothly with distance (`spread_deg` + `spread_growth_deg_per_m` beyond `close_range`, capped by `max_spread_deg`; per-stream pattern scales `stream_spread_scale`), and per-stream damage falls from `close_range_damage` (0.6) to `damage_per_stream` (0.25) by `max_range` (26 m): 5/5 streams and 3.0 damage up close (kills a robot), ~4 at 10 m, ~2 at 14 m, 1 beyond 18 m. At medium range (from `assist_min_range`) the outer streams (`assist_min_scale`) already passing within `assist_cone_deg` of another enemy within `secondary_search_radius` of the target bend towards it (`assist_strength`, curved stream, one per enemy per shot) for `secondary_damage_multiplier` damage. Flaming `ShotgunFlash` (flame tongue per barrel, white-hot fireball, glow, sparks, smoke, orange light; `muzzle_flash_intensity`), orange PlasmaFx impacts. `fire_rate` 1.1/s. Self-contained: works in either slot alongside any weapon. `shotgun.glb` = supplied `Shotgun_v2.glb` simplified 71k -> 21k vertices (visually identical); source excluded from export.
- Shotgun trails are railgun-style (`ShotgunStream` + `scripts/vfx/rail_helix.gdshader`): per stream a camera-facing beam (white-hot additive core + alpha-blended orange glow, so it stays orange and distinct over bright floors), a spiral ribbon mesh wound round it (built once per shot, revealed by the travelling head) and sparkling motes; after the hit the whole trail lingers 0.7 s while the spiral widens and fades. Muzzle flash (`ShotgunFlash`): a long three-layer flame tongue per barrel, rolling fireball, white-hot flare, wide glow, flame licks that burn on ~0.4 s, sparks, smoke and a bright orange light (energy 9, range 7). Flame sprites `muzzle_01/04/05`, `flame_01/03`, `fire_01`, `star_09` come from the supplied weapon VFX pack (Kenney particle pack, CC0), converted to 256 px greyscale.
- Second pad (`ShotgunSpawnPad`, at (-4, 0, 1)) uses the same pad prefab with `glow_color` orange: `WeaponSpawnPad.glow_color` recolours the pad's purple (model colour + emissive textures via `scripts/world/glow_recolor.gdshader`, no extra textures; ring and halo tints). The Black Hole Generator pad is unchanged. Pickups go into the RIGHT slot (no weapon-changing system yet).
- Machine Gun (`scenes/weapons/machine_gun.tscn`, `MachineGun`, `resources/weapons/machine_gun.tres`): arm-mounted like the cannon/shotgun (ARM_END, same sockets, mirrored per arm). `machine_gun.glb` = supplied `Raper_V1.glb` simplified 112k -> 31k vertices with gltfpack (`-si 0.2 -slb -kn -km -noq`; source `machine_gun_source*` kept, excluded from export); `Muzzle_Exit` sits between the front claws on the bore line. Yellow pad `MachineGunSpawnPad` (same prefab, `glow_color` yellow) north of the central tower; pickup -> RIGHT slot, then in the wheel for either arm.
  - **Firing**: yellow plasma rounds (pooled `PlasmaBolt`, flickering core, orange trail) from the muzzle, exactly along the barrel (straight at the target when the barrel is within `aim_snap_deg`). The barrel itself is kept on the target: the gun feeds its measured aim error back to its arm (`aim_correction`, via `CharacterAnimator.aim_offset`, zero for every other weapon), which also holds down the recoil climb. Rate climbs from `base_fire_rate` (9/s) to `max_fire_rate` (24/s) over `spin_up_time` (2.4 s) of continuous fire, winds down over `spin_down_time`; several rounds per tick are spaced along the line at high rates.
  - **Heat**: `heat_per_shot` (0.9 of `max_heat` 100) per round; cools at `cooling_rate` once it stops firing for `cool_delay`. Full-rate fire overheats it after ~5.5 s: firing stops for at least `overheat_lockout` (1.6 s) and until the heat is back under `resume_heat` (30 %), with a burst of yellow steam, sparks and arcs.
  - **One shared value**: `energy` (0..1; spin takes it to 0.7, heat to 1) drives every effect (`MachineGunEnergy`): transparent orb with a pulsing core, plasma swirl and arcs inside (glass drawn without a depth pre-pass so the inside shows); plasma packets flowing through the conduits to the barrel (`plasma_conduit.gdshader`: yellow/white packets, hot orange/red edges, faster/turbulent with energy, unstable dropouts near overheating); front/barrel heat glow creeping back from the muzzle yellow -> orange -> red (`barrel_heat_glow.gdshader`, additive overlay only while warm); yellow `BarrelStatic` arcs/sparks round the barrel with heat; yellow-tinted steam once hot. Tunables: `barrel_glow_intensity`, `electric_intensity`, `steam_amount`, `orb_energy_intensity`, `conduit_energy_intensity`, `muzzle_flash_intensity`.
  - **Muzzle flash** (`MachineGunFlash`, one per gun): every round restarts it (crossed flame tongues, white-hot core, glow, tiny arcs, a few sparks from a fixed 18-spark pool, the gun's single light pulsing); each flash lasts 32 ms and is always shown for at least one frame, so high rates read as a rapid flash sequence.
  - **Impacts**: pooled `PlasmaFx` in yellow at `impact_scale` 0.6: flash, ring, yellow/orange sparks, crackling arcs, a mark that fades in `impact_mark_life` (0.45 s), and on every other hit a brief light from the shared 4-light pool (no light per round).
  - **Recoil**: each round kicks the arm spring by `recoil_strength` (0.06) x (1 + `recoil_spin_boost` x spin), so rapid fire builds a sustained, bounded push on that arm and the chest (peak ~0.26 vs ~0.8 for a shotgun blast), plus a fast mechanical shake of the gun itself (`vibration`). Per arm; both guns keep fully separate spin/heat/recoil/effects.
- Black Hole Generator: `scenes/weapons/black_hole_generator.tscn` (gun GLB, optimized 28 MB → 10 MB) spawns `scenes/weapons/black_hole_core.tscn` (separate, unmodified animated black-hole GLB, normalized to 1 m) at the gun's `Black_Hole_Projectile_Spawn` marker. `detach_core()` is there for firing later.

## Weapon wheel and dual-wield loadout
- `WeaponRegistry` (`resources/weapons/weapon_registry.tres`): every weapon in wheel order. Each `WeaponDefinition` has id, display name, icon (`tools/render_weapon_icons.gd` renders `assets/ui/weapon_icons/<id>.png` from the weapon scene), accent colour, `unlocked_at_start` and `allowed_sides`. A new weapon = definition + registry entry (+ icon render); no UI changes.
- `WeaponLoadout` (on the player): session unlocks (only the Default Cannon at start), `equip(side, def)`, `available_for(side)`. Pads: the first pickup unlocks the weapon and puts it on the RIGHT arm; a pad for an already unlocked weapon is left alone (it's in the wheel). Nothing is ever removed.
- Switching (`WeaponSlot.switch_to`, ~0.3 s): the old weapon stops firing and retracts into the arm (scales into its mount) with an energy burst in its colour (`WeaponSwitchFx`: flash, ring round the arm, arcs, sparks), then the new weapon snaps out of the same WeaponSocket with a small overshoot and fires as soon as it is half out. Only that arm is affected; its target lock stays, so the new weapon takes over the target at once.
- `WeaponWheel` (`scripts/ui/weapon_wheel.gd`): one compact HUD button (shows both arms' weapons) in the touch cluster. Hold it: the wheel opens in the middle (left half = LEFT ARM, right half = RIGHT ARM, centre = what each arm holds / will hold), the game slows to x0.5 (movement keeps working on the other thumb). Drag towards a weapon (direction from where the thumb went down; 48 px dead zone, sideways band to pick an arm, hysteresis) and release to equip it on that arm; release near the start to cancel. Highlights: hovered arm, hovered weapon (its colour, name), equipped weapons (tick). Desktop: hold Tab, move the mouse.

## Tap-to-target auto fire
Four separate, reusable pieces (any weapon/enemy can use them):
- `Targetable` (`scripts/targeting/targetable.gd`) – put on anything lockable, at its aim point. Forgiving tap area (`select_radius`, `select_half_height`), `kill()/revive()`, stable `target_id` for networking.
- `TargetSelector` (local player only) – turns a tap into a target: screen-space hit area around each enemy (at least `min_tap_radius` px), closest to the tap wins. Per-slot rules: first enemy → RIGHT; another enemy → LEFT (both busy: the slot locked longest ago switches); double tap (same enemy within `double_tap_window` 0.35 s) → BOTH lock it; tapping an enemy both share unlocks LEFT first, then RIGHT; tapping an enemy only one slot has unlocks only that slot.
- `TargetLock` (on every player; `TargetLock` = RIGHT, `TargetLockLeft` = LEFT via its `side`) – holds the target sent in `PlayerCommand.target_id` / `target_id_left`; clears on death / invalid / beyond `max_range` (40 m) and emits `target_lost`, which clears only that slot's selection.
- Auto-fire per slot – while locked, a slot fires whenever its weapon's barrel is within `fire_cone_degrees` of its target and `weapon.can_fire()`; each weapon sets its own rate/projectile/damage/effects via `fire_at()`. Both slots fire simultaneously.
- Weapon fit (any weapon in any arm): every held weapon's bounds in its forearm frame (`WeaponSlot.fit_box`) go to `ArmAimModifier`, which predicts both weapon boxes from the posed bones each frame and, while anything would touch (weapon/weapon, weapon/other arm, weapon/torso; `fit_margin`), spreads the arms apart, stacks crossed arms over/under (small weapons only; taller than `fit_bulky_height` never cross), moves an idle arm out of an aiming arm's way, or turns the upper body further for an aiming arm reaching across the chest (`fit_max_spine_deg`). Corrections apply at once and relax slowly. Mounts start in front of the elbow and recoil can never slide or tilt a weapon past it (`WeaponSlot.MIN_REAR_GAP`). `WeaponBounds` has the oriented-box maths; tests check all combinations (cannon/shotgun/BHG pairs) in shared, split, crossed and running poses.
- Upper body: `ArmAimModifier` aims each armed arm at its own target (parallel aim lines for a shared target), twists the spine (±60°) towards the average; hips/legs keep following movement. Procedural limits instead of physics keep the cannons apart: an arm aims at most `max_inward_yaw` (40°) across the chest, and when the targets are crossed the right arm lifts and the left drops (`cross_stagger_deg` 14°) so they pass over/under each other (≥ 0.15 m clearance in tests). Recoil is per arm (`recoil_side`), the chest leans with the sum (both firing = stronger) and twists towards the side that fired. Standing still, the player turns to face the average of its targets.
- UI: lock-on rings (`scripts/ui/target_marker.gd`): RIGHT magenta, LEFT cyan, both rings on a shared target. The old FIRE button and crosshair are gone.
- Test dummies are enemies: 5 hits to destroy, respawn after 4 s.
- `BlackHoleGenerator.fire_at()` releases the chamber core as a `BlackHoleProjectile` (same node, animation keeps playing; disk turned to face back along the flight path). It flies straight, stops on the first hit (calls `on_projectile_hit` if the collider has it), collapses and frees. A new core grows in the chamber after `recharge_delay` (12 s) over `recharge_grow` (3 s): 15 s between shots.
- `BlackHoleProjectile.impacted` is the hook for gravity / damage / supernova later.
- Armed (always, with the default cannons): camera eases to an over-the-shoulder offset.

## VFX (Black Hole Generator)
`scripts/vfx/` – visual only, no gameplay logic. Textures: 10 greyscale sprites from Kenney's Particle Pack (CC0, `assets/vfx/`, 52 KB total) tinted at runtime with additive unshaded materials; CPUParticles3D (WebGL-safe).
- `MuzzleFlash` – crossed flame quads along the barrel, star pop, brief purple light.
- `ChamberArcs` – flickering lightning strips between the contained core and the containment ring (only while charged). The chamber also has a small, subtle distortion disc (`chamber_lens_size`/`chamber_lens_strength` on the gun) that fades out before the chamber frame.
- `BlackHoleFlightVfx` – gravity distortion (`gravity_distortion.gdshader`, screen-texture lens/twist), event-horizon ring, counter-rotating swirls, matter spiralling in, crackles. `Vfx.distortion_enabled` turns the distortion off if a device struggles.
- `ImpactBurst` – flash, scorch star, expanding shock ring, sparks, light pop (also used as the expansion pulse).
- `BlackHoleLightning` – pooled procedural lightning (14 small + 4 large bolt meshes, jagged forking camera-facing ribbons) lashing outward; each strike casts one ray (biased down/sideways) and lands on real surfaces/enemies when in reach (more likely the nearer they are), with pooled flashes, sparks and one shared light.
- Fired projectile: stays chamber-sized until `clear_distance` (1.4 m), then surges (ease-out with overshoot + pulse) to `flight_size` 3 m. Collision is a sphere sweep with `collision_radius` = 22% of the diameter; `influence_radius` (1.2x diameter) is there for gravity later.

## Recoil (Black Hole Generator)
Sequence: `fire_at()` starts a 0.18 s charge (core swells/shudders, arcs frenzy, chamber lens surges) → launch → `Weapon.recoiled` → `WeaponHolder` kicks the `CharacterAnimator` recoil spring (fast kick, rebound, settle) and emits `weapon_recoil` (the camera no longer reacts; `CameraRig.kick()` still exists if wanted later).
- `ArmAimModifier` applies the spring additively: firing arm's elbow driven back + forearm/muzzle climb; chest (Spine2 only) leans back and twists. Hips/legs and movement untouched.
- `WeaponHolder._align_weapon` slides the weapon back along its barrel and pitches the muzzle up with the same spring.
- Gun mechanics: `Front_Muzzle_Deep_Bore` slams back and rebounds; `Finished_Containment_Interior` spins 120° per shot.

## Black hole impact → gravity well → supernova
`BlackHoleProjectile` states: FIRED → TRAVELLING → STUCK_GRAVITY_WELL → COLLAPSING → SUPERNOVA → FINISHED (flying full range without a hit just fizzles).
- On hitting anything it sticks at the contact point for `gravity_well_duration` (5 s), pulsing irregularly; instability ramps up suction, spin (core animation speed_scale), lightning violence, particles and distortion. At most `max_active_wells` (3) at once; the oldest collapses early.
- `GravityWell` (`scripts/weapons/gravity_well.gd`): one sphere query every 0.15 s on the **movable** physics layer (layer 2: props, enemies, players), nearest `max_affected_objects` kept. Clamped inward/orbital/total speeds, mass resistance (sqrt(mass/ref)), lift near the core, visual shrink/spin via the body's mesh children (original transforms stored; body scale never touched). Inside `capture_radius` bodies are captured: collision off, kinematic, tiny orbit in the core (max `max_captured_objects`).
- Players: `PlayerController.external_velocity` – the pull is added on top of normal movement (capped at 8 m/s; sprint escapes). Supernova shoves via `apply_external_impulse`.
- Collapse: rapid shrink to a pinpoint + brief bright compression, then `SupernovaBlast` (flash, two shock rings, distortion bubble, sparks, light) and `GravityWell.supernova()`: captured bodies released one per physics tick at spread, ray-checked positions with restored collision/size, ONE capped launch each (`max_launch_speed`), radial damage (`take_damage`), brief camera kick for nearby local players.
- Tunables on the projectile: gravity_well_duration, gravity_radius, gravity_strength, player_gravity_strength, capture_radius, orbit_strength, shrink_rate, pulse_amount, pulse_speed, supernova_radius, supernova_damage, supernova_launch_force, maximum_affected_objects, maximum_captured_objects, max_active_wells.
- Arena: dummies are now heavy upright RigidBody3D enemies; `Props/` has light crates, barrels and a heavy block (`PhysicsProp`).

## Black hole particles
While travelling (and while stuck) `BlackHoleFlightVfx` adds purple glow motes and black dust orbiting the disc (local space) and a dark purple mist (`assets/vfx/smoke_puff.png`, alpha-blended via `Vfx.mix_material`) shed into the world so it trails and tumbles behind.

## Phones in the browser
`Vfx.configure_for_device()` (called by Main): on a touch device in the browser, effect lights (muzzle flashes, impacts, blasts - every `OmniLight3D` goes through `Vfx.tame_light`) light nothing. iOS WebGL drew objects those lights touched solid black (and they are expensive there); the flash sprites still show. `?lights=1` turns them back on for testing. The black holes' gravitational lens stays on everywhere (it is most of their look); `?lens=0` turns it off. Testing switches: `?weapon=<id>` starts with that weapon in the right hand (e.g. `black_hole_generator`, `shotgun`, `machine_gun`), `?autolock=1` keeps the nearest enemy locked.

## Skirmisher robot (light, fast, agile)
`scenes/enemies/robot_skirmisher.tscn` / `scripts/enemies/robot_skirmisher.gd` (`RobotSkirmisher extends RobotEnemy`): the first robot's physics body, targeting, line of sight, spacing, damage, death styles, break-apart, debris, corpse and respawn are reused as they are; only its model, movement, evasion and weapons are its own. ## Sealed open meshes (caps)
Removed hands (player gauntlets) and cut body sections (robot) left open holes you could see through. `MeshCaps` (`scripts/world/mesh_caps.gd`) seals them with the minimum geometry, built offline by `tools/build_mesh_caps.gd` (source GLBs untouched): it welds vertices by position (UV seams aren't holes), finds closed boundary loops across all surfaces of each mesh, and writes a shallow fan cap per hole (centre slightly recessed, both windings, skinned like its rim). At load the caps are merged into an existing surface of each mesh (no extra draw calls, existing LOD index buffers extended) and the result is shared by every instance.
- Player (`grinch_caps.res`, 3 KB): the two gauntlet openings, using the existing material and a dark texel of its texture (104 cap triangles, both windings). Applied by `CharacterAnimator.mesh_caps`.
- Robot (`robot_caps.res`, 9 KB): the model already contains interior caps for its section cuts (`Hidden_Dark_Mechanical_Interior`, authored invisible with `activate_opacity_on_detachment`); they're now drawn opaque (shared material copy - cheaper than the old invisible transparent surface). The five cuts it lacked (left shoulder on torso and upper arm, left hip on pelvis and thigh, under the pelvis) get generated caps in that same surface. Living robots look identical (they're hidden inside the joints); corpses and every debris piece show solid dark cut faces.
- Rebuild after changing a model: `godot --headless --path . -s tools/build_mesh_caps.gd`.

## Character mesh reduction
`grinch.glb` (110k -> 74k vertices) and `robot_enemy.glb` (108k -> 60k) were simplified with gltfpack (`-si 0.6` / `-si 0.2 -slb -kn -km -ke -ac -af 0 -noq`: named nodes/sections, materials, skins and the 28-bone rigs kept), then `tools/transplant_animations.py` copied the ORIGINAL animation data back in byte for byte (gltfpack thins keyframes), so every pose of every clip is identical to the source.
- **Living robots draw as one mesh**: `RobotEnemy` merges the 15 section meshes (same skin, same 2 materials, same skeleton) into one shared 2-surface mesh, built once on the first spawn (~30 ms desktop). Sections stay loaded but hidden; `die()` switches back to them before any breakup, so death/breakup behave exactly as before. 9 robots in view: 401 -> 86 draw calls; pixel output identical (max 1/255 rounding).
- **Distance and shadows**: beyond `far_distance` (30 m, 2 m hysteresis, Godot visibility ranges) a living robot draws `robot_enemy_far.glb` (9.5k vertices, made by `tools/make_robot_far.py`, textures stripped, reuses the main materials) instead of the 60k one; its shadow is always cast by that simple mesh (`ShadowProxy`, shadows-only) while the detailed body casts none. Removed on death with the merged mesh.
- **Stress test**: open the game with `?stress=30` (or `-- --stress=30`) to replace the arena robots with N patrolling robots and show frame time, draw calls, triangles, robots and memory in the HUD; `?perf=1` shows the numbers only.

## Hybrid lock-on combat 3.0
Aiming and firing stay automatic; skill comes from movement, timing, target choice and weapon mix. Each mechanic can be switched off on its own.
- **Firing solution and cover** (`TargetLock`): `line_of_sight`, `cover_memory` 0.3 s, `los_interval` 0.1 s (one ray per lock from eye height; only level geometry - static bodies - counts as cover, not robots/props/players). The moment cover hides the target, weapons hold fire (`WeaponSlot.update` needs `has_clear_shot()`); visible again within the 0.3 s grace (a pillar sweeping past) and firing resumes; hidden longer and the lock turns off ("obstructed") and isn't re-taken until the player taps again.
- **Out of view**: a locked target off screen (behind the player or past the screen edge, `screen_margin` 2 %) for more than `off_screen_grace` 0.25 s turns the lock off too ("off_screen"; the player's camera is `TargetLock.view_camera`, set in `main.gd` for the local player); not re-taken until tapped again. The existing barrel-alignment cone (25 deg), 40 m lock range and weapon ranges still apply. No camera snapping or forced turning. Rings (`TargetMarker`, colours unchanged): firing = 4 bright arcs; behind cover = 8 dim dashes + an arc draining as the memory runs out; lost = the ring shrinks and fades where the target was.
- **Perfect dodge** (`scripts/combat/perfect_dodge.gd`, one per player): on a dodge, each enemy bolt in flight is projected along its real path; it qualifies only if it would have hit the body (`hit_radius` 0.55) within `window` 0.18 s. The reward is granted only after that same bolt (`PlasmaBolt.serial`, carried in `DamageInfo.projectile_serial`) is confirmed to have missed; each bolt rewards once. Reward: `counter_duration` 2 s of +`stagger_bonus` 35 % stagger (no raw damage, no invulnerability, dodge unchanged), refreshes rather than stacks; electric surge on both cannons, `perfect_dodge.ogg` (generated), "COUNTER" by the health bars. Boss missiles don't count yet (only bolts: robot cannons, skirmisher cannons, boss chaingun).
- **Concentrated fire / stagger** (`scripts/combat/stagger_meter.gd`): tougher enemies only - the boss (`stagger_threshold` 9, `stagger_duration` 1.25 s, `stagger_armour_scale` 3x armour damage while staggered). Hits add damage x weapon factor (plasma 1, machine gun 0.45, shotgun 1-2x by closeness, black hole 1.5) x 1.25 when the same player's other arm hit within 0.4 s x the counter bonus; drains after 1 s without hits; 3 s immunity after a stagger (no stun-lock). Staggered: chaingun and missile wind-up interrupted, stands still, shudders with sparks. Light troops get no meter (quick kills); both arms on one simply kills it twice as fast, with stronger instability sparks (HitFeedback).
- **Split fire / suppression** (`scripts/combat/suppression.gd`, light troops, `RobotEnemy.suppressible`): when a player's arms are on two different targets, that player's hits build suppression (plasma 0.14, MG 0.07, shotgun 0.12, black hole 0.3 per hit; drains 0.45/s after 0.5 s). Aim spread grows up to 3x; past 0.5 the current burst is cut short and the next delayed 0.7 s with a flinch - at most every 2.5 s per enemy. Movement and dodging are never affected.
- **Readable tells**: the skirmisher's cannon glow swells `windup_glow_time` 0.35 s before each burst.
- **Co-op ready** (no networking yet): counter/perfect-dodge state lives on each PlayerController; stagger and suppression are one shared object per enemy fed by any player's hits; concentrated bonus is per player (their own two arms); hits carry `DamageInfo.arm`; nothing assumes one local player.

## Robot arm aim (outstretched, steady)
`RobotArmAim` holds both arms out straight like the player's while a robot fights: collarbone, forearm and hand take their rest pose (the walk/run clips' arm swing no longer moves them) and the upper arm is set so each cannon's muzzle points along the line to the target, its rest "up" side kept up (no rolling or bouncing). Each arm stays within `arm_cone_deg` (50) sideways and `arm_pitch_deg` (60) up/down of where the chest faces (after the upper-body twist), so arms never swing back across the body; when the target is outside that, `on_target()` is false and the robot holds fire (bursts wait) until it has turned. The skirmisher's barrel glows are placed on the `posed` signal, the moment the arms are posed each frame, so they sit exactly on the cannon ends.

## Robot walking (no gliding, flat feet)
- **Clip speed = ground speed**: the walk/run clips play at body speed divided by the measured ground speed of their planted feet (`tools/measure_gait.gd`: grunt walk 0.98 / run 2.59 m/s, skirmisher walk 0.94 / run 2.12 - the old values were 20-35 % off, so the feet skated), between `anim_rate_limits`; walk/run switch at `run_above` with hysteresis. Below `stand_below` (0.15 m/s) they stop stepping and stand (`Stand`: the walk clip's both-feet-down pose, made at runtime).
- **Grunt - planted clip feet** (`scripts/enemies/robot_foot_ik.gd`, `RobotFootIK`, a SkeletonModifier3D before the arm aim): stride warping (the clip's steps are stretched/shortened along the real velocity so the planted foot moves back exactly as fast as the body moves), foot locking (a foot on the ground - low, and carried backwards by the clip - stays fixed in the world until it lifts), flat feet (a planted foot is laid flat on the floor under it, one ray per foot, ramps followed), two-bone leg IK; the hips drop a little if a leg can't reach.
- **Skirmisher - procedural legs** (`scripts/enemies/robot_walker.gd`, `RobotWalker`): its retargeted clips bowed and crossed its legs and rolled its feet, so its thighs, shins and feet are posed from a step cycle instead (the clip still drives hips, torso, arms). Cadence 1.0 -> 2.3 cycles/s and planted share 60 % -> 34 % (flight phase) with speed up to `run_speed` 4.6; planted feet locked and flat; swinging feet arc (`lift` 9-20 cm) to where they'll be under the hip mid-stance (from the velocity, so turns, backpedals and stops land right), toes dipping at lift-off and rising before landing; feet `stance_width` 0.29 m either side, knees forward (`knee_out` slightly outward); crouch and step bob grow with speed. Standing it stops with both feet down; a foot left out of place (stop, turning on the spot) takes a settling step. **One clock for the whole body:** the walk / run clip (hips, torso, arm swing) is played locked to these steps (`RobotWalker.clip_time`, `RobotSkirmisher.CLIP_MID_LEFT` - where each clip's left foot is mid-stance), so the body bobs and sways with the steps actually taken. The skirmisher's step rate is set for its legs (`cadence` 0.9 -> 2.1 cycles/s, about one leg length per step at a full run). The legs stay procedural through turns: turn actions follow the turn clips' heading curves while the walk / run / stand clip plays, and turning on the spot steps each foot to where the body will be facing (`settle_cadence`). When a jump / dive hands the legs back, the feet start from where the clip has them (no pop); the jump hands back at touchdown (`jump_land_time`), the dive ends as its clip lands (`dive_clip_range` 0.2-1.62 s; past that the clip hovers in a horizontal lunge). Sidesteps, jumps and dives swing the facing round at `snap_turn_rate` (14 rad/s) instead of in one frame. **Corpses:** the death clips were made for a human whose arm ends on the floor; this robot's forearm cannon props the body up like a stand. When the clip ends the body settles over onto the floor as one rigid shape (`_settle_corpse`: a temporary convex body built from the posed armour's outline, centre of mass at the hips; the clip, rig and pose are untouched). **Broken-off parts** collide with their own outline (`BreakApart.hull_shapes`: per bone, the part's outermost vertices along 26 directions, posed as it breaks) instead of a bounding box - a box round the long, diagonal arm was far bigger than the arm, so a detached arm landed on the box with the arm hovering near the body. Skirmisher only. Broken-off parts and the settling corpse also pass through robots' and players' movement capsules (`_ignore_characters`; invisible, and at twice size much wider than the body), so an arm can't come to rest against one in mid-air. **Dying, the arms are kept tucked in** (owner's choice): the human death clips fling the arms high and wide, and this robot's upper arm is a thin rod, so the cannon looked detached and floating beside the shoulder. During deaths only, each upper arm is held to at most `death_arm_raise_max` (50 deg) from hanging down (`_limit_arms`, applied to the pose right after the clip each frame via `mixer_applied`, so the settling corpse and any arm that breaks off use the same pose); the rest of the clip plays as it is. **No jumping** (owner's choice, `jumps_enabled = false`): evades are sidesteps and dives only; the jump code is kept, unused. **Every death the right arm breaks off** at the shoulder as its own piece and falls to the floor (owner's choice; `_schedule_arm_break`, same timing as other break-offs, or with the first batch when the hit breaks more). **Joints sealed:** each section's open edge (shoulders, hips, neck, waist) has a cap; the living robot draws them too (`_whole_mesh_skips` is false), so a bent joint shows a dark cap instead of a see-through gap.
- Measured (tests): planted feet slide 0.000 m at 0.5-4.6 m/s (was 0.1-0.9 m/s); skirmisher feet never cross. Off beyond 40 m from the camera (too small to see). Jumps, dives, turn clips and deaths fade it out.

## Hit reactions and impact feedback (light robots)
`HitFeedback` (`scripts/combat/hit_feedback.gd`), one per `RobotEnemy` / `RobotSkirmisher` (not the boss, which has its own). Layered on top of the clips; it never restarts an animation or changes damage, AI or speeds.
- **Which weapon**: `DamageInfo.weapon` (`&"plasma"`, `&"machine_gun"`, `&"shotgun"`, `&"black_hole"`) is set by `PlasmaBolt.weapon_tag` (machine gun sets its own), the shotgun streams and the black hole's direct hit; supernova damage counts as the black hole.
- **Severity**: `damage / max_health + force / force_scale` -> LIGHT / MEDIUM (`medium_at` 0.25) / HEAVY (`heavy_at` 0.5); a close shotgun stream (impact force >= 6) is always HEAVY. With today's numbers: machine-gun round = light, plasma bolt = medium, close shotgun / black hole = heavy.
- **Per hit**: the boss's additive reaction (`BossHitReact`, now with `bone_name` and a `max_kick_speed` cap so rapid fire can't stack) leans the upper body away from the shot on a stiff spring and shakes it (runs before the arm aim, so the cannons stay on target); a 50-80 ms additive edge glow over the body in the weapon's colour (one shared shader, one material per colour, no per-robot copies); `JointSparks` at the nearest joint (medium+: sometimes a second joint a moment later). Per weapon (`PROFILES`): plasma = medium jolt, cyan glow, sparks every hit; machine gun = small twitch, sparks at most every 0.18 s, yellow flicker on about half the rounds at most every 0.1 s; shotgun = big jolt, orange glow, one large spark burst + metal scrap per volley; black hole = heavy, purple.
- **Heavy hits**: `_hit_stagger()` - the skirmisher does its short stagger (if not mid-move; `stagger_cooldown` 1.2 s, so no stun-lock); the first robot gets the jolt only. The skirmisher's own body jolt/stride check now scales with severity, so machine-gun fire no longer stops it (it keeps ~3 m/s under full-rate fire).
- **Gravity wells**: while a well pulls or holds a living robot (`in_gravity_well()`), it shudders, crackles and glows purple every 0.25-0.55 s.
- **Death**: a large spark burst at the killing hit in the weapon's colour, a spray of 6-12 metal chips (`ScrapChips`: opaque CPUParticles boxes, 6 shared emitters, 0.8 s, no physics), and the electrical failure (`RobotDeathSparks`, now for the first robot too) - sizes randomised each time. Existing death clips, break-apart, corpse and recovery shards unchanged.
- **Cost**: beyond `fx_distance` (32 m) only the lean and glow run. Pools: JointSparks 16, ScrapChips 6, RobotDeathSparks 4, PlasmaFx as before; no new lights.

## Health, armour and recovery shards
- **Player** (`PlayerController`, Health group): `max_health` 100, `max_armour` 100 (`start_armour` 0). Armour soaks hits before health (`armour_absorb`). Enemy damage is in robot-health units, so it is scaled by `incoming_damage_scale` (5: grunt bolt 5 HP, skirmisher bolt / boss chaingun 2 HP, missile up to 15 HP). The player's own supernova never hurts them. At 0 health (`can_die`) the player respawns at spawn with full health; signals `vitals_changed`, `died`. `heal()` / `add_armour()` never go past the maximum.
- **Shards** (`scripts/combat/recovery_drops.gd`, `RecoveryDrops`, one per scene, made on first use): every light robot (`RobotEnemy` / `RobotSkirmisher`, `drops_recovery`; not the boss) drops `drops_per_kill` (1) shard on death: green health (`health_value` 8) or blue armour (`armour_value` 6) - whichever of the player's health/armour is lower as a share of its maximum. Shards burst out, bounce, skid to a stop and hover; within `magnet_radius` (3 m) they fly to the player and are collected within `collect_radius` (1.5 m) - but only by a player who can use them (full health = health shards stay). They blink out after `lifetime` (10 s). Pooled plain meshes moved in code (one floor ray at spawn, no physics bodies, no lights; one unshaded crystal + one additive glow quad each), at most `max_active` (16; the oldest is recycled). Cleared when the player respawns.
- **Kill streak**: `streak_kills` (3) kills within `streak_window` (5 s) make shards worth +`streak_bonus` (25 %, and a little bigger) for `streak_duration` (6 s).
- **HUD** (`scripts/ui/vitals_hud.gd`): two thin bars with numbers top-left under the FPS line (health green, armour blue), flashing on pickup, plus "RECOVERY +25%" while the streak bonus runs.

## Robot combat
- **AI** (`RobotEnemy`, Combat group): finds the nearest player (group `players`) within `detect_range` (28 m, lost beyond 40 m), checks line of sight every 0.25 s (other robots don't block), fights from `preferred_range` 7–14 m: advances/backs off, strafes and switches sides every 1–2.4 s, flips away from walls, keeps apart from other robots, moves like the player character: legs face where it's going (Walking/Running by speed) while `RobotArmAim` twists the spine (up to `max_twist_deg` 105°) to keep chest and cannons on the target; moving away from the target it faces it and walks backwards (Walking reversed, `backpedal_speed_factor`). Helpless while a gravity well holds it. `RobotEnemy.ai_enabled` turns all robot AI off (tests).
- **Aiming**: `RobotArmAim` (SkeletonModifier3D) straightens the forearms and turns the shoulders so both cannon muzzles (green tips, `MUZZLES` in hand-bone space) point at the player, blended over the playing clip; it records the aimed muzzle positions for firing.
- **Firing**: bursts of 3–4 shots alternating left/right cannon (`burst_interval` 0.12 s), 1.3–2.3 s between bursts, 0.5–1 s reaction after spotting, slight lead and 2° spread, `fire_range` 26 m.
- **PlasmaBolt** (pooled 64): 30 m/s, one ray cast per tick (no physics body), flies through robots, damages via DamageInfo (players get `apply_damage` → hit count + green screen-edge flash; no health yet), nudges props.
- **PlasmaFx** (pooled): green muzzle flash + brief green light (max 4 lights shared), impact flash + ring + green sparks, and on level geometry a glowing green scorch mark that crackles with tiny arcs/sparks and fades in ~1.3 s.
